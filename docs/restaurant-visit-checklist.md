# Restaurant visit checklist (idea 20)

The restaurant module (`sites/comida/content/restaurants/`) only publishes what was seen or confirmed. Use this on each visit, then fill a copy of `_template.mjs`.

1. **Basics (from the restaurant's own sign, menu or social profile):** name, address, neighbourhood, opening hours with the date you checked them, reservation method.
2. **What you ate:** 2–4 dishes, what they were like, portion size. No scores, no star ratings, no "the best in Asunción".
3. **Setting:** noise, space for groups or children, parking, accessibility.
4. **Photos:** only your own or ones the restaurant gave permission for, with alt text and the caption "Imagen ilustrativa" if they are not from the visit.
5. **Prices:** do not publish amounts (AGENTS.md rule 5). You may describe "económico / intermedio / para ocasiones" in words.
6. **Sources:** set `source` to "Visita de comida.com.py el <date>" and `verifiedAt` to the visit date. Re-check hours every 6 months.
7. **Monetisation:** featured placement is allowed later, but must be labelled as such on the page.

Targets from the KWP data: restaurantes en asuncion (6,600), lugares para comer/cenar/almorzar en asuncion (590–720), cuisine + asunción (170–590), churrasquería (4,400), parrilladas (1,900).
