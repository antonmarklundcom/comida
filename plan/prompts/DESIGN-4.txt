Design the home page of comida.com.py, a Paraguayan referral service for catering and chef a domicilio in Gran Asunción. Visitors describe their event in a short form; the service reviews it and introduces checked catering providers who quote, contract and invoice directly. Audience: two groups on one page, office managers and HR staff booking corporate events (coffee breaks, fin de año dinners, almuerzos), and families booking bodas, fiestas de 15 and cumpleaños. Most visits are Android phones on mobile data. All UI text in Paraguayan Spanish with voseo (Contanos, Pedí, Elegí, Escribinos).

Deliver one artifact with two frames stacked vertically: a desktop frame 1440 wide on top, then the same page as a mobile frame 390 wide directly below it. Same content in both, not a cropped version.

Hard content rules, these are legal and business constraints, do not soften them:
never show a price, a number of guaranteed quotes, a response time in hours, the words verificados or garantizado, or coverage beyond Gran Asunción;
no testimonials, star ratings, review counts, client logos or provider names;
image areas are soft tinted blocks with the caption Imagen ilustrativa, no faces;
WhatsApp is the secondary action everywhere, the form is the primary action.
Locked tokens for this direction, use exactly these values:
display font Bricolage Grotesque 600 (fallback Archivo Black), text font Inter (fallback system sans);
base #F6F1EA, ink #1C1917, ember #B7391F for primary CTAs and one highlight per screen, surface #FFFFFF, charcoal #2A2623 for the two dark full-bleed sections, smoke #8A817A for secondary text;
tints for section fills and cards only: bone #EFE6D8, ash #E6E1DA, ember-tint #F3DCD3;
WhatsApp green #25D366 only inside the WhatsApp button glyph, never as a fill;
type scale ratio 1.28 from 17px body, H1 clamp(46px, 6vw, 80px) line-height 0.98 weight 600 with tight letter-spacing -0.02em, eyebrows 12px uppercase letter-spacing 0.14em;
radii 4px inputs, 10px cards and images, 20px feature panels; borders at 12 percent ink opacity; grain overlay on both dark sections; no gradients except a bottom scrim on images.
