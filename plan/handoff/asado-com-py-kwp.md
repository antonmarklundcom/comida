# asado.com.py: keyword data from the comida.com.py KWP research

Generated 2026-09-25 from `plan/research/kwp-all.csv` in the comida repo (antonmarklundcom/comida). Source: Google Keyword Planner, 6 rounds pulled by Anton on a Swedish Google Ads account and **targeted at Paraguay (Spanish)**. That is why CPC is in SEK. Volume = average monthly searches in Paraguay, as KWP buckets it (e.g. 1,900 / 2,400 / 2,900 are buckets, not exact counts).

- Meat, grill and fish keywords in the file: **429**, together about **139,580 searches a month**.
- The column "comida page" shows where comida.com.py already targets that exact phrase. See the cannibalisation rules below.
- Brand, restaurant and "cerca de mí" rows are listed for completeness only. Do not build pages to rank for other businesses' names.

## How to split asado.com.py and comida.com.py (avoid cannibalisation)

comida.com.py is a general food hub. Asado is not its first priority: it keeps its existing meat pages (32 cuts, meat guides, meat recipes, asado catering) but should not grow deeper into grilling. asado.com.py should become the deep grilling authority:

| Topic | Owner | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Grilling technique: fire, coal vs wood, points of doneness, times per cut, parrilla/quincho equipment, asadito grills, chimichurri and grill sauces | **asado.com.py** | Pure asado intent; comida only has a short "asado a la parrilla" guide |
| Cut encyclopedia for the grill (every beef, pork, lamb and achura cut, with grill method) | **asado.com.py** (deeper), comida keeps its pages | Write *grill-first* angles ("vacío a la parrilla: tiempo y punto") instead of comida's general cut explainer ("qué es el vacío") |
| Oven and stove meat recipes (asado a la olla, estofado, milanesa, matambre a la pizza, canelones) | **comida.com.py** | Kitchen recipes, not grilling |
| "Cuánta carne por persona" and asado calculator | Both, different angle | comida: party planning plus catering. asado: grill-master planning (cuts by grill time, coal per kg). Link to each other. |
| Asado catering / asado para eventos | **comida.com.py** (lead forms, VenderCRM) | asado.com.py links to it for event leads |
| Butchers, carnicerías, meat prices | **asado.com.py** directory later | Commercial local intent |

Cross-link rather than duplicate. When asado.com.py covers a phrase comida already targets, give it a clearly different H1/intent, and consider a canonical or a short comida page linking to the deeper asado.com.py page.

## Keyword tables

- **vol** = monthly searches in Paraguay. **CPC low/high** in SEK (top-of-page bid range from KWP; blank = not shown).

- "dorado" appears in KWP with mixed intent (the river fish and brands/restaurants).
- "ala" in phrases like "matambre ala pizza" is a real Paraguayan misspelling people search. Target it naturally inside the page text, never as a heading.
- "hamburguesas cerca de mi" (8,100) is a delivery or restaurant intent, not an asado topic.

## Using this in the asado repo

1. Copy this file into the asado repo as `plan/research/kwp-asado.md`. The machine-readable subset is `asado-com-py-kwp.csv` next to this file.
2. Pull a fresh KWP round for asado-only seeds before writing: parrilla, quincho, carbón, leña, punto de la carne, tiempos de cocción, parrilla a gas, parrilla de obra, cortes por nombre, achuras. Most rows here came from food/meat seeds and miss grill-equipment long tails.
3. Keep the comida rules: no invented facts, no prices unless sourced and dated, food-safety temperatures (pork 63 °C with rest, poultry 74 °C, ground meat 71 °C).
