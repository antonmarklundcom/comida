# KWP round 3: seed lists (written 2026-09-24, Opus 5.5)

Purpose: size a fresh-food delivery line (vegetables, fruit, cheese, meat, eggs) sourced from the
Mercado de Abasto and delivered to Asunción, Fernando de la Mora, Luque, San Lorenzo, Lambaré and
the rest of Gran Asunción; and finish the open questions from `plan/11` §6 (viandas fitness, meat
commerce).

## Settings (save these next to every export)

- Account: Anton's Google Ads account (Swedish UI, bids in SEK). Location and language are what matter.
- **Run A:** location Paraguay, language Spanish, Google Search network, last 12 months.
- **Run B:** same, location limited to Asunción + Departamento Central (or the individual cities
  Asunción, Fernando de la Mora, Luque, San Lorenzo, Lambaré, Mariano Roque Alonso, Capiatá, Ñemby,
  Limpio if the location picker offers them). Run B tells us how much of the demand sits inside the
  delivery radius.
- **Download the CSV** (Ladda ned sökordsidéer), do not paste. The CSV carries the 12 monthly columns,
  which gives seasonality (fin de año, Semana Santa chipa, asado season).
- Save as `plan/research/kwp-round3-<list>-<A|B>.csv`.

Two ways to use each list: put the **10 discovery seeds** into "Discover new keywords" (KWP takes 10
at a time) to get ideas, and paste the **volume check** list into "Get search volume and forecasts" to
get numbers for exact terms the ideas may miss.
