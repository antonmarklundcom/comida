### Counting and classification assumptions

Volume totals below are monthly KWP bucket sums, not measured unique searches. Raw sums count every row. Stem-deduplicated sums count one demand for each class + normalized intent stem + identical volume bucket, including matches across lists. Different volume buckets remain separate even for the same stem, as requested; consequently these totals are not an independent market-size estimate. No buckets are merged solely because they have equal volume.

Normalization removes accents and function words, singularizes common terms, ignores word order, and groups obvious spelling or intent equivalents. Recipe, ingredient, quantity, preparation style, location, and price modifiers remain distinct except for explicit synonyms. The five sopa paraguaya variants count as one demand at 8100. Generic restaurant and nearby restaurant variants at 33100 count as one demand, consistent with the existing summary. Every actual merge is listed below for audit.

Classification uses query wording and list context, without external brand verification. Catering includes bocaditos and event-planning queries. Meat-cut includes generic meats and meat types as well as named cuts; meat-butcher-brand includes generic carniceria. Meat-cooking takes preparation queries from either recipe or meat lists. Honey-commerce is reserved for explicit price or pure-honey queries; generic honey varieties stay informational. Supermarket offers include prices, discounts, and promotions; generic supermarket discovery goes to other. Restaurant brands take precedence over nearby or editorial modifiers. Restaurant-editorial includes locations, cuisines, generic dining formats and where-to-eat queries. Generic restaurant discovery and explicit proximity go to restaurant-nearme. Unclear menu, reservation, equipment, translation and delivery queries go to other. No chef query occurs.

Flagged: carni and punto carne are provisionally butcher brands; picanha grill and name-like restaurant queries such as brasas, el gallo, temaki sushi and restaurant italia are provisionally restaurant brands. These intent assignments do not verify business identities. Pure honey is a weak commerce signal. Location and account settings were not verified. The previous summary remains unchanged; its earlier rough estimates are not substituted for the parsed totals.
